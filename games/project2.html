Technophage

Technophage is an innovative 2D/3D platformer that introduces a unique projection mechanic. Players can utilize special screens throughout the levels to project their image onto walls and surfaces, creating new pathways and solving environmental puzzles.

The game blends traditional platforming challenges with mind-bending spatial reasoning puzzles, requiring players to think in both two and three dimensions simultaneously.

The projection system allows players to overcome obstacles that would be impossible through conventional platforming alone. By strategically placing projections, players can activate switches, create platforms, and manipulate the environment in creative ways.

Each level introduces new mechanics and challenges that build upon the core projection system, ensuring the gameplay remains fresh and engaging throughout the experience.

Technophage features a striking visual style that clearly distinguishes between the real world and projected elements. The art direction uses color and lighting to guide players and highlight interactive elements within each level.

Developed with Unity, Technophage pushes the boundaries of what's possible in platformer design, offering a unique take on spatial puzzle-solving that will challenge even experienced platformer fans.
