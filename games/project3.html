Flesh Trail

Flesh Trail is an intense top-down arcade-style game where players must continuously reach objectives while fending off endless waves of zombies. The core gameplay loop focuses on high-score chasing and efficient pathfinding through increasingly dangerous environments.

Players are equipped with various weapons and abilities that can be upgraded between rounds, allowing for different strategic approaches to surviving the zombie hordes.

The game features fast-paced combat with tight controls that reward precision and quick decision-making. Each zombie type has unique behaviors and attack patterns, requiring players to adapt their strategies on the fly.

As players progress, the zombie waves become larger and more varied, introducing new challenges that test both combat skills and resource management. The scoring system rewards efficient zombie elimination and quick objective completion.

Flesh Trail's visual style uses bold colors and clear visual feedback to ensure players can quickly parse the chaotic battlefield. The user interface is designed to provide essential information at a glance without cluttering the screen.

Built with Unity, Flesh Trail delivers smooth performance even during the most intense zombie swarms, ensuring the gameplay remains responsive and enjoyable throughout extended play sessions.
