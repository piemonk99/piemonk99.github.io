Hazzardous

View on GitHub

Hazzardous is a roguelike turn-based strategy game that combines deep tactical gameplay with unique build options. Players navigate through multiple hand-crafted levels, facing increasingly challenging enemy encounters while collecting new upgrades and abilities.

The game features an intricate final boss battle that tests all the skills and strategies players have developed throughout their run. Each playthrough offers a new variety of abilities with unique modifiers, ensuring no two runs are ever the same.

The combat system in Hazzardous emphasizes strategic selection of an ability loadout and careful use of them in combat.

Hazzardous was developed over 6 months using Unity, with a focus on creating maintainable, expandable backend systems that allow for myriad updates in the future.
