Hazzardous

Hazzardous is a roguelike turn-based strategy game that combines deep tactical gameplay with unique build options. Players navigate through procedurally generated levels, facing increasingly challenging enemies while collecting powerful upgrades and abilities.

The game features an intricate final boss battle that tests all the skills and strategies players have developed throughout their run. Each playthrough offers new combinations of abilities, ensuring no two runs are ever the same.

The combat system in Hazzardous emphasizes strategic positioning and ability synergies. Players must carefully manage their action points and cooldowns while anticipating enemy movements and attacks.

With over 50 unique abilities and 30 different enemy types, Hazzardous offers deep strategic complexity that rewards careful planning and adaptation to unexpected situations.

The progression system allows players to unlock permanent upgrades and new starting options, providing meaningful long-term goals while maintaining the challenging roguelike experience.

Hazzardous was developed over 6 months using Unity, with a focus on creating tight, responsive controls and visually distinct ability effects that clearly communicate game state to the player.
